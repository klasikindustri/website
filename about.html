Klasik Industri Sdn Bhd Description

KLASIK INDUSTRI SDN BHD can be described as a professional commercial kitchen solutions provider, offering reliable equipment and customized solutions for restaurants, hotels, cafés, catering businesses, bakeries, and other food-service establishments.

Our company's goal is to provide high-quality, durable, energy-efficient, and practical kitchen equipment that helps customers improve productivity while maintaining food safety and hygiene standards.



Industry Services

*Equipment supply and sales
*Kitchen design and planning
*Equipment installation
*Custom stainless-steel fabrication
*Kitchen renovation
*Equipment maintenance and repair
*Replacement parts
*Ventilation and exhaust systems
*Complete commercial kitchen setup
